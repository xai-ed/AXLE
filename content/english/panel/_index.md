# Suggested questions for the panel
suggested_questions:
  - "What does it actually mean for an educational AI system to be explainable to the stakeholders it affects?"
  - "Is the goal of XAI in education to build trust, or to cultivate distrust in the AI supported system? Is this distrust always healthy and is it enough to ensure agency?"
  - "Who carries the burden of explainability, and who should? Does the answer change based on who the explanation is designed for?"
  - "How does the rise of generative AI affect the explainability and agency challenges in education?"
  - "What would it mean for educational institutions, not only AI developers, to take responsibility for explainability?"
